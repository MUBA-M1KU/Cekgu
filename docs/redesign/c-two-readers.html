How It Works

Four steps, and you make the last one.

Cekgu is a first pass, not a vetting committee. It never changes a key, never certifies a paper and never grades anyone. Every decision on this page is yours.

ABCD

01

You Type the Paper

Questions, options and the key you intend to publish. Nothing is sent until the whole set is valid.

02

Two Readers Answer Blind

Two different model families sit each question as a candidate would. Your key is withheld from both, so neither can agree with it out of politeness.

AB

03

The Rules Fire

Two verified readings agreeing on a non-key option is a Possible Key Error. Fewer than two is Unverified, not a guess.

✓

04

You Decide

Correct the key, revise the wording, confirm it as written, or dismiss the flag. Cekgu records which.

Trust and Privacy

What Cekgu does with your paper.

Your key is never sent to a model

Each reader receives the stem and the options only. That is the whole reason two readers agreeing against your key means anything at all.

Every reading has a receipt

Each attempt carries the Gonka request id the gateway issued, and the id resolves to a receipt you can open. A reading whose receipt cannot be verified does not count toward the two-reader floor.

Records are yours, and they expire

Private records are kept 3 months from the last time you opened or changed one. Deleting a record puts it in Trash for 30 days. Deleting everything is immediate and is in Settings.

Cekgu is a first pass

Agreement between models is not proof of truth. Cekgu does not certify a paper, change a key, or grade anyone, and it is not a substitute for a moderation panel.
