Why the key is withheld

One AI gives you one opinion, and it will happily agree with the answer key you show it. So Cekgu never shows it. Each reader sees the stem and the options and nothing else, and answers as a candidate would.

Two different model families are used rather than the same model twice, because one model asked twice is one opinion with extra steps.

Cekgu is a first pass, not a vetting committee, and agreement between models is not proof of truth. It does not certify a paper, change a key, or grade anyone.
