Two readers, or Cekgu says nothing

One AI gives you one opinion, and it will happily agree with the answer key you show it. Cekgu withholds your key and asks two different model families to answer independently.

Where fewer than two independent readings survive verification, the item is marked Unverified rather than guessed. A reading whose request id cannot be resolved to a receipt does not count, so the floor is enforced by the gateway rather than by trust.

Cekgu is a first pass, not a vetting committee. Agreement between models is not proof of truth, and it does not certify a paper, change a key, or grade anyone.
