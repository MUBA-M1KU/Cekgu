## 00:14 — Sui (gold sponsor) & track reveal

**[00:14:57] Richard:** Sui is a Layer 1 blockchain developed by **Mysten Labs**, using an object-centric architecture and the **Move** programming language — the foundational infrastructure layer for high-throughput decentralized applications, enabling ultra-low-latency asset transfers.

**Sui judge & mentor:** **Rafael** — Solutions Engineer, Mysten Labs (also the representative speaker today).

**[00:16:31] Rafael:** _"Sui is a Layer 1 — for those new to Web3: Ethereum, Bitcoin are Layer 1s; Sui is a Layer 1 as well. I'm a Solutions Engineer at Mysten Labs, almost a year now. Day to day I empower and enable builders."_

### Sui Track 1 — Payments & Stablecoins

- Build the future of **money movement** — anything involving sending, receiving, managing money.
- Example ideas: **campus payments**, **creator payouts**, **tipping services** (like YouTube/Twitch tipping).
- Looking for: solves a **real payment or financial workflow**; **fast, simple, intuitive UX**.
- _"We don't want somebody to come to the website and realize it's too difficult to onboard. Part of what makes Sui special is that we focus a lot on user experience."_
- Relevant Sui features: **sponsored transactions**, **zkLogin**, **PTBs** (programmable transaction blocks).
- **Prizes:** 750 / 500 / 250 USD

### Sui Track 2 — AI + Sui

- Anything AI powered by Sui: **AI agents with on-chain wallets**, **AI assistants executing agentic payments**, **agent-to-agent commerce marketplaces**.
- _"I know this sounds very futuristic, but this is where we are heading — it's going to be a great step for agentic commerce."_
- Helpful features: **zkLogin**, **wallets**, **PTBs**, **sponsored transactions**.
- **Prizes:** 750 / 500 / 250 USD

### 🎯 Rafael's judging criteria (4)

| #   | Criterion                       | What he means                                                                                      |
