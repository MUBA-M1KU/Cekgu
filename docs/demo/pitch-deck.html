2. Project objective

She reads three questions instead of twelve.

Cekgu uses two separate AI models on GonkaRouter to audit every answer, so the educator only reads the ones that came back flagged instead of all of them.

Cekgu

3. Overall concept

Two GonkaRouter models answer without seeing the answer key. A fixed rule compares them.

Neither model is shown the teacher’s answer or the other model’s. The rule checks whether the two agree first, and only then whether they agree with the answer key.

4. Technology and track

One server, one gateway, two models.

One Bun process, one gateway, four calls in flight, and a rule you can read off the screen.

  1. 1Submit

    Checked locally. The record exists in under a second, before any model runs.

  2. 2Queue

    Postgres. One item claimed at a time with SKIP LOCKED. Four gateway calls in flight.

  3. 3Two models

    Both through GonkaRouter, neither shown the answer key, no fallback allowed on any call.

  4. 4Receipt check

    GET /v1/receipts/{id}. The served model must equal the one asked for, within 5 s.

  5. 5Fixed rule

    Five verdicts, in a fixed order. Fewer than two verified answers means Unverified.

  6. 6Record

    Verdict, every attempt, every request id. Her decision is saved next to it, not instead of it.

5. Motivation and challenges

Two separate models on every question, and we wait for both.

With two models on GonkaRouter, no verdict rests on one model’s mistake. A second answer takes time, so the queue does the waiting and the teacher does not.

So Cekgu queues the work, retries, and marks the question Unverified rather than guessing.
